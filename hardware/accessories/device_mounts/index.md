This guide provides comprehensive information about the available mounting options for your AutoPi device. Choose the appropriate mount type for your installation environment, secure your device safely in vehicles with different interior configurations, and access downloadable 3D-printable mount designs for customized installations.

## Mounting Options

### Flat Surfaces Mount
The mount is meant to be screwed or glued to a flat surface and to snap on the bottom of the device. 

![Device mount for flat surfeces](/img/hardware/accessories/device_mounts/device_mount_3_cut.png)

Download mount for flat surfaces.

### Curved Surfaces Mount
The mount is meant to be glued or secured with zip ties to a curved surface such as a support pipe under the dash or a curved piece of plastic and to snap on the bottom of the device.

![Device mount for curved surfeces](/img/hardware/accessories/device_mounts/device_mount_curve_1_CutV2.png)

Download mount for curved surfaces.
