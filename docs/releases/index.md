# Releases

Welcome to our monthly releases. Here you'll find curated summaries of the most important updates to the AutoPi platform, organized by month.

## What You'll Find Here

Each release page highlights:

- **New Features** — major improvements and capabilities added to the platform.
- **Bug Fixes** — stability improvements and issue resolutions.
- **Screenshots & Descriptions** — visual guides and clear explanations to help you understand what's new.
- **Where to Find It** — step-by-step navigation to locate features in the cloud interface or device settings.

## Our Updates

Releases may include updates to one or more of the following:

- **Frontend** — Cloud UI and dashboard.
- **Backend** — Cloud API and services.
- **Core** — device firmware and core libraries.

## Want Technical Details?

Looking for a complete, unfiltered list of all changes? Visit the Changelog section for raw technical details pulled directly from our development repositories.

## Need Help or Have Questions?

- **Support Issues** — if you encounter any issues or bugs, please contact our support team at [support@autopi.io](mailto:support@autopi.io).
- **Feature Requests & Sales Inquiries** — questions about features or interested in discussing potential new capabilities? Reach out to our sales team at [sales@autopi.io](mailto:sales@autopi.io).
